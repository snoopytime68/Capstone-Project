Two cousins of mine brought me to their friend group and absolutely did NOT introduce me to anyone, which is how I made more friends for the first time and eventually found my best friend on my own. Now I am more social and I try to work harder on character faults and of course school.

A Problem I Solved

Difficult problems often require interesting solutions. Keep working. You’ll find your way. For example, my younger brother ran away from me at school. He was super emotional because he couldn’t say goodbye to his friend. My goal was to get my brother to be happy and follow me to the car without an issue. I started going through a list of ways to succeed and everything I could imagine would theoretically fail and make things worse. I eventually came up with an idea. I take my phone out, go to my contact list and select myself. So that way when the text happens I will get it instead of someone else. Then I tell my brother that you can text your friend goodbye. The risk was that he knew how to read my name. But at his age I didn’t believe he could do that. It worked. He came home much happier and way more compliant. Overall finding creative solutions that are truly outside the box can find your way to a much easier life.

My Personality

I took the 16Personalities test and my result was Logician (INTP-T). I tend to be creative and chase new knowledge so I can understand everything. I love to start new projects, but I struggle to finish them. I am also not always comfortable in social situations, which is something I am working on.

Habits of Mind

  1. Thinking Flexibly: When the obvious answer won’t work, I look for creative solutions, like the trick I used to get my brother to the car or new ways to design my 3D models.
  2. Striving for Accuracy: In 3D modeling and printing, my measurements and clearances have to be exact or the pieces won’t fit together, like the hollow shafts in my nested stars model.
  3. Questioning and Posing Problems: Before I can solve a problem, I need to ask questions and understand what is really going wrong, whether it’s a bug in my code or bad readings from our VEX robot’s motors.

Projects & Activities

Project

Splicer (Work in Progress)

3D modeling is actually more complicated than you may think. The easy part is imagining what you want in 3 dimensions with moving parts and how it works. Then the hard part is translating thought to software to 3D printer. It is much harder because you need to learn how to model, how to make the clearance your part requires and all the mechanism required for the model to work in the real world.

Nested Stars 3D Model

I designed a 3D model of stars nested inside each other, organized by size. All of the stars connect with screws and hollow shafts, so each smaller shaft fits through the larger one. Getting the parts to fit together took careful planning of sizes and clearances, and I am proud of how it came together.

Personal Statement Essay

I open up my computer, pull up the assignment, and start doodling on a piece of paper. I check the clock. The assignment is due at 11:59 PM. I got plenty of time at 6 PM to get this assignment done. I don’t get on any video games though because I still want to get this done. Hands go to my keyboard and I type some words. I check the clock and it is now 10 PM. Sliding back the chair, I get up and go make myself a drink and come back and now it’s 11 PM. There is still plenty of time to do this assignment. I sit down and go to Google. I searched for some interesting starships for my favorite video game and now it’s 11:35 PM. Freaking out, my hands fly across the keyboard as I check the time. Okay, it is now 11:45, now it’s 11:47. I speed up, not caring about how bad the grade is, since a missing assignment is worse than done. The clock just turns to 11:59 PM. Closing my computer and standing up, I prepare to go to bed. Now it’s somehow 1 AM and I’ve done nothing to get ready for some sleep, and I wake up at 6 AM.

I got into a conversation with a friend about how I struggle a lot with getting the assignments done on time. “You know,” my friend said in the tone that meant it was an obvious solution, “you can just listen to some music when you work.” I have thought of that actually. I tell him my dislike of music. “Just try some music from a game you play.” I go home thinking and very skeptical about it. I’ll try anyway. Pulling up YouTube on Brave, which thank goodness blocks ads or I wouldn’t try this, I play music from Harry Potter, Minecraft, Lies of P, and Star Citizen. None of them kept me in focus long enough to make a meaningful dent. Then I tried Doom Eternal music. I didn’t want to try this one because I wasn’t a fan of heavy metal. But I tried anyway. I glance at the clock. 4:32. I start typing out my 300-word essay. 150 words in, I glance at the clock. 4:49. I go back to work feeling eager. I finished at around 432 words and turned it in at 5:21. I spend the rest of my time playing video games, proud that I got the homework assignment done in record time.

Afterwards the quality of my assignments improved and overall I have been doing better in school. I’ve always thought that I wasn’t smart enough or fast enough to keep up with the other students here at Windsor Charter. But I can, and I have so much more time to do things that I would have been unable to do quickly because of my ADHD. I learned how important it was to work first before getting the enjoyment you crave.
