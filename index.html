Senior Capstone Project

Difficult problems often require interesting solutions. Keep working. You’ll find your way. For example, I have a younger brother with autism running away from me at school. He was super emotional because he couldn’t say goodbye to his friend. My goal was to get my brother to be happy and follow me to the car without an issue. I started going through a list of ways to succeed and everything I could imagine would theoretically fail and make things worse. I eventually came up with an idea. I take my phone out, go to my contact list and select myself. So that way when the text happens I will get it instead of someone else. Then I tell my brother that you can text your friend goodbye. The risk was that he knew how to read my name. But at his age I didn’t believe he could do that. It worked. He came home much happier and way more compliant. Overall finding creative solutions that are truly outside the box can find your way to a much easier life.

Splicer (Work in Progress)

3D modeling is actually more complicated than you may think. The easy part is imagining what you want in 3 dimensions with moving parts and how it works. Then the hard part is translating thought to software to 3D printer. It is much harder because you need to learn how to model, how to make the clearance your part requires and all the mechanism required for the model to work in the real world.
