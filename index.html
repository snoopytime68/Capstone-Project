How I Have Grown

I was a crazy antisocial person since entering my freshman year in this high school. Two cousins of mine brought me to their friend group and absolutely did NOT introduce me to anyone, which is how I made more friends for the first time and eventually found my best friend on my own. Now I am more social and I try to work harder on character faults and of course school.

A Problem I Solved

Difficult problems often require interesting solutions. Keep working. You’ll find your way. For example, my younger brother ran away from me at school. He was super emotional because he couldn’t say goodbye to his friend. My goal was to get my brother to be happy and follow me to the car without an issue. I started going through a list of ways to succeed and everything I could imagine would theoretically fail and make things worse. I eventually came up with an idea. I take my phone out, go to my contact list and select myself. So that way when the text happens I will get it instead of someone else. Then I tell my brother that you can text your friend goodbye. The risk was that he knew how to read my name. But at his age I didn’t believe he could do that. It worked. He came home much happier and way more compliant. Overall finding creative solutions that are truly outside the box can find your way to a much easier life.

Projects & Activities

Project

Splicer (Work in Progress)

3D modeling is actually more complicated than you may think. The easy part is imagining what you want in 3 dimensions with moving parts and how it works. Then the hard part is translating thought to software to 3D printer. It is much harder because you need to learn how to model, how to make the clearance your part requires and all the mechanism required for the model to work in the real world.

Activity

VEX

VEX involves coding a robot to complete a task. It is also a difficult task because you often have to deal with really bad and cheap components that create a lot of inaccurate readings on the motors. Other skills you need are creative reasoning, teamwork, and some serious patience.

Coursework I Am Proud Of

Dr. Innocenti’s Essay

Completing Dr. Innocenti’s essay is one of the things I am most proud of. It was a really hard assignment, and I stayed after school for long hours working on it with him. Finishing it showed me that I can push through something difficult when I put in the time and ask for help.

Nested Stars 3D Model

I designed a 3D model of stars nested inside each other, organized by size. All of the stars connect with screws and hollow shafts, so each smaller shaft fits through the larger one. Getting the parts to fit together took careful planning of sizes and clearances, and I am proud of how it came together.
